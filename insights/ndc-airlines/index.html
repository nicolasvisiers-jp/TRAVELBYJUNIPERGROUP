NDC & Airlines

NDC & Airlines: The New Distribution Capability Explained

Travel by Juniper Group · 6 min read

NDC — New Distribution Capability — is an IATA XML standard that enables airlines to distribute rich, differentiated content directly to travel agencies and OTAs without the constraints of legacy GDS infrastructure. After a decade of development and controversy, NDC is now a mainstream reality that every travel technology company must navigate.

The traditional GDS model — where airlines distributed availability and fares through Amadeus, Sabre, or Travelport — served the industry well for decades. But it has structural limitations: GDS fare formats are restrictive, ancillary selling is constrained, and airlines struggle to personalize offers or distribute bundled products effectively. NDC was designed to solve these problems by enabling airlines to own the offer creation layer.

Under NDC, airlines expose their inventory through modern JSON/REST APIs (an evolution of the original XML standard) that allow rich offer creation — dynamic bundles, personalized fares, real-time ancillary pricing, and seat-level content. Travel agencies and OTAs access this content through NDC-certified aggregators and connectors.

For travel agencies, NDC creates both opportunity and complexity. On the opportunity side: richer content, airline-direct fares unavailable on GDS, and the ability to sell ancillaries with genuine value. On the complexity side: servicing (changes, cancellations) is more complex with NDC bookings, and agencies need technology that can handle both GDS and NDC content in a unified workflow.

Lleego and InteRES, within the Travel by Juniper Group ecosystem, are specialists in multi-source air aggregation — combining GDS and NDC content into unified search results for agencies and OTAs. This aggregation layer is essential for agencies that want NDC content without the operational burden of managing multiple direct airline connections.

The NDC landscape is still maturing. Servicing capabilities (post-booking modifications) continue to improve as airlines invest in their NDC platforms. The next phase will likely see NDC content reach full feature parity with GDS for most use cases — at which point the shift in distribution economics will be fully felt across the industry.
