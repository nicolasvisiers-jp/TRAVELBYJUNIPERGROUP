Juniper Travel Technology

Juniper Travel Technology is the founding company of the ecosystem — a full-stack travel technology platform born in Palma, Spain in 2003. It provides one of the most comprehensive travel booking engines in the European market, alongside an XML connectivity gateway that links buyers and suppliers across accommodation, flights, transfers, activities, and dynamic packages.

Products & Capabilities

What Juniper Travel Technology Does

🔗
Booking Engine
Multi-channel booking platform supporting B2B, B2C, and white-label configurations for travel agencies, tour operators, and OTAs.
📡
XML Connectivity
High-throughput XML gateway connecting buyers to hundreds of accommodation, flight, and activity suppliers through a single integration.
📦
Dynamic Packaging
Real-time package creation combining hotel, flight, transfer, and activity content into bookable travel products.
🏨
Hotel Direct Connect
Direct XML connectivity to hotel chains, bed banks, and channel managers for lowest-cost-in-channel access.
✈️
Flight Integration
GDS and NDC-compatible flight content aggregation for multi-source air booking within the platform.
🌐
White Label
Fully brandable storefronts enabling travel brands to deploy their own booking experiences powered by Juniper's infrastructure.
