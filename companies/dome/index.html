Dome

Dome is a specialist software company founded in Palma in 2002, serving destination management companies (DMCs) with purpose-built operational and distribution software. Its platform covers the full DMC workflow: ground operations management, transfer and excursion booking, supplier contract management, and B2B distribution to tour operators and wholesalers worldwide.

Products & Capabilities

What Dome Does

🗺️
DMC Operations Platform
End-to-end platform managing the full DMC workflow from supplier contracting through service delivery and invoicing.
🚌
Transfer Management
Reservation and logistics management for airport transfers, private hire, and group transportation services.
🎭
Excursion Booking
Booking and operations management for tours, excursions, activities, and guided experiences offered by DMCs.
📋
Supplier Contracts
Contract management module handling allotments, rates, special deals, and supplier payment terms.
🔗
B2B Distribution
Integration with tour operators and wholesalers enabling DMC content to flow directly into upstream booking platforms.
📊
Operations Analytics
Reporting and business intelligence for DMC managers on booking volumes, revenue, supplier utilization, and margins.
