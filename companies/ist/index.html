IST

IST is a veteran cruise technology company founded in Barcelona in 1999. With over 25 years of experience, IST provides the connectivity and distribution infrastructure that cruise lines use to reach travel agents and OTAs worldwide. Its systems handle booking, inventory management, and agent tools for the cruise sector — making it a critical piece of the cruise distribution chain in Europe and beyond.

Products & Capabilities

What IST Does

🚢
Cruise Distribution
End-to-end cruise booking distribution infrastructure connecting cruise lines to travel agents and online channels.
🔌
API Connectivity
Robust APIs enabling cruise line inventory and pricing to flow in real-time to agent booking platforms and OTAs.
🏢
Agent Booking Tools
Purpose-built cruise booking interfaces for travel agents, including cabin visualization, availability calendars, and quote management.
📋
Inventory Management
Real-time inventory and allocation management tools for cruise lines to control cabin availability across all distribution channels.
🌍
Multi-Market
Distribution capabilities spanning European and global markets with multi-currency, multi-language support.
🔗
Ecosystem Integration
Native integration with other Juniper Group ecosystem companies including Traveltek for combined cruise and package distribution.
