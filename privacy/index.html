Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Travel by Juniper Group ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us (such as when you contact us), as well as information collected automatically when you visit our website (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited via standard web analytics).

How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to respond to your enquiries, improve our website, and better understand how our content is used. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Cookies
Our website uses essential cookies to function properly and analytics cookies to understand visitor behaviour. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

Data Retention
We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@juniper-group.com.

Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

Each portfolio company operates its own privacy policy governing data collected through their respective products and services.
