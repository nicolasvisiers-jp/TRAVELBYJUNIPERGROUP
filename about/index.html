Building the Infrastructure
of Global Travel

Juniper Group is not a fund. It’s not a holding company that optimizes for exit. It’s a permanently committed ecosystem of travel technology companies — each one a market leader in its niche — united by shared values, shared connectivity, and a shared commitment to the long-term health of the global travel industry.

Global ecosystem — and growing

Juniper Group began with Juniper Travel Technology, founded in 2003 in Palma, Spain. What started as a booking engine company quickly became one of the most connected travel technology platforms in Europe — linking tour operators, OTAs, and travel agencies to hotel, flight, and dynamic package content.

Over the following two decades, the Juniper Group ecosystem grew — not through financial engineering, but through a disciplined focus on vertical market excellence. Each acquired company was a specialist. Each one stayed independent. Each one kept its brand, its team, its mission. Today, 14 companies and counting.

In 2020, Juniper Group became part of Vela Software — the travel and hospitality vertical of Constellation Software Inc. (TSX: CSU). This partnership gave Juniper Group the permanent capital, governance structure, and global network to accelerate its ecosystem strategy without ever compromising on what makes each company great.

The result is a unique asset in the global travel technology landscape: a family of companies that collectively cover the entire value chain — from booking engine to connectivity middleware, from hotel channel management to cruise APIs, from AI-powered hotel mapping to destination management operating systems.

What We Stand For

Our Principles

Four commitments that shape every decision we make — from acquisition to operations, from investment to integration.

🔒

Permanent Partnership

We never sell the businesses we acquire. Our commitment is measured in decades, not years. Founders who join Juniper Group receive a permanent home — not a stepping stone to an exit they didn’t want. Our ownership horizon is generational.

🎯

Operational Excellence

We invest in great products, great teams, and customer success — not financial engineering. Each company in our ecosystem is supported to be the best version of itself, with the resources and autonomy to serve its customers at the highest level.

🤝

Ecosystem Intelligence

14 companies sharing market intelligence, distribution networks, and technical connectivity creates compounding value that standalone companies cannot replicate. Our ecosystem makes every member stronger — without erasing what makes each one unique.

🌎

Global Perspective

From Latin America to Asia, Europe to North America, we operate in 100+ countries and understand local travel markets deeply. Our geographic diversity is not incidental — it is a deliberate strategy to build a truly global travel technology infrastructure.

Our Structure

Part of Something Larger

Juniper Group operates within a world-class ownership structure built for permanent, patient capital. Every level of this chain shares the same philosophy: acquire great businesses, keep them forever, invest in their long-term success.
