Our Philosophy

Built for the Long Run

Not private equity. Not a financial holding. A permanently invested travel technology ecosystem with one mission — building world-class vertical software for the global travel industry.

♾️

Forever Home Philosophy

We never sell our businesses. Every company that joins Juniper Group receives a permanent home with long-term capital, operational support, and the stability of Constellation Software backing (TSX: CSU). Your company, your brand, your people — protected for generations.

🔑

Full Operational Autonomy

Every portfolio company retains complete operational autonomy. Founders and leadership teams continue making day-to-day decisions, maintaining customer relationships, and driving product strategy. We provide capital and expertise — never interference.

🌐

Ecosystem Network Effects

Joining Juniper Group means joining a network of 14+ interconnected travel companies. Shared API connectivity, cross-portfolio integrations, joint customer relationships, and strategic synergies that make every company stronger as the ecosystem grows.

🏔️

Travel-Only Specialization

Unlike generalist acquirers, we operate exclusively in travel technology. Our depth across distribution, connectivity, hospitality, cruise, air, and destination tech creates unique operational value, deeper customer relationships, and stronger compounding returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Concise answers about Juniper Group, our travel technology ecosystem, and our approach.

What is Juniper Group?
+
Juniper Group is a global ecosystem of vertical market travel technology companies, operating as part of Vela Software — a Constellation Software (TSX: CSU) company. The group acquires and builds software businesses across the travel industry: distribution platforms, booking engines, connectivity solutions, hospitality software, cruise technology, air aggregation, destination tech, and AI/data tools. Companies operate autonomously while benefiting from shared ecosystem value and permanent capital.
What companies are in the Juniper Group ecosystem?
+
Juniper Group's 14-company portfolio includes: Juniper Travel Technology (booking & connectivity), Peakwork (dynamic packaging), Cangooroo (LATAM distribution), Vervotech (AI hotel mapping), IST (cruise distribution), Roibos (hotel B2B marketplace), Alturos (destination OS), Lleego (flight aggregation), Traveltek (dynamic packaging & cruise), Dome (DMC software), Hotetec (hotel channel management), InteRES (air aggregation), RezMagic (cruise event management), and Inspiretec (agency CRM & reservations).
Is Juniper Group a private equity firm?
+
No. Juniper Group is not a private equity firm. It is a permanent, operationally-engaged travel technology ecosystem operator. Unlike PE, Juniper Group never sells businesses, never imposes exit timelines, and prioritizes long-term product quality over financial engineering. Part of Constellation Software (TSX: CSU) — a public company with a 30-year track record of acquiring and permanently holding vertical market software businesses.
What travel technology layers does Juniper Group cover?
+
Juniper Group covers 8 critical travel technology layers: Distribution (booking engines, XML aggregators, dynamic packaging, B2B marketplaces), Connectivity (API infrastructure, GDS, NDC), Hospitality (channel management, CRS, hotel inventory), Cruise (cruise APIs, booking, event management), Air & Mobility (flight aggregation, NDC, GDS), Destination Technology (DMO platforms, DMC software), AI & Data (hotel mapping, NLP, content enrichment), and Agency Technology (travel CRM, reservation systems, portals).
How does Juniper Group support travel technology acquisitions?
+
Juniper Group acquires vertical market travel technology businesses where founders seek long-term stability. We provide permanent capital, deep travel-industry expertise, global ecosystem connectivity (14+ companies, 100+ countries), and the stability of Constellation Software backing. Travel technology founders retain full operational autonomy, brand identity, team continuity, and customer relationships after joining the group.
What is Constellation Software's relationship to Juniper Group?
+
Constellation Software Inc. (TSX: CSU) is the ultimate parent company of Juniper Group. CSI operates through six operating groups including Vela Software — which owns Juniper Group. Since 1995, CSI has acquired 1,100+ vertical market software businesses across 100+ countries and has never sold one. This permanent ownership model flows down to every Juniper Group portfolio company.
