Acquisition Criteria

What Makes a Great Fit

We've built a highly focused travel technology ecosystem. Our acquisitions are deliberate — we look for businesses that deepen the group's capability and strengthen its collective value.

🏚
Vertical Focus
Travel technology companies with deep domain expertise: booking engines, connectivity platforms, hospitality software, cruise tech, air aggregation, destination technology, or agency tech. Domain depth matters far more than scale.
💰
Recurring Revenue
SaaS, subscription, or transaction-based revenue models. Profitable or near-profitable businesses preferred. EBITDA positive is a plus, but great businesses at earlier stages are welcome — we invest in potential, not just current performance.
🌎
Global or Regional Scale
Companies serving travel businesses across any geography — from single-market leaders with deep local dominance to global platforms spanning multiple continents. We value market leadership over market size.
👥
Strong Team
Founders and management who care deeply about their product, their customers, and their people. We invest in teams, not just technology. A company that has earned customer trust is a company worth building for the long run.
How It Works

Simple. Respectful. Fast.

We've designed our acquisition process to respect founders' time and dignity. No unnecessary complexity, no drawn-out uncertainty. Just clear, honest conversations at each stage.

1
Initial Conversation
A confidential, no-obligation call with the Juniper Group team. 30 minutes. No pitch deck required — just tell us about your company, your vision, and what you're looking for. We'll share honestly about who we are and how we work.
30 minutes · Confidential · No commitment
2
Mutual Fit Assessment
We learn your business, you learn our approach. We share case studies from our existing portfolio companies, talk through your questions, and — if it makes sense — introduce you to other Juniper Group founders who have been through the process.
1–2 weeks · Transparent · Meet the ecosystem
3
Non-Binding Letter of Intent
If there's mutual interest, we issue an LOI within 2–4 weeks of initial discussions. Our terms are simple, fair, and founder-friendly. We don't negotiate in bad faith or use the LOI as a tool to reduce price during diligence.
2–4 weeks · Founder-friendly terms · Fair valuation
4
Due Diligence
Efficient and focused. We know travel technology deeply — we won't ask you to explain what a booking engine is. Our diligence is purposeful, respectful of your time, and targeted at understanding your business on its own terms, not against a generic template.
4–8 weeks · Travel-native team · No unnecessary friction
5
Close & Join the Ecosystem
Welcome to Juniper Group. Your team stays. Your brand stays. You keep building. From day one, you have access to 13 sibling companies, shared infrastructure, group buying power, and the backing of Constellation Software — while running your business exactly as you choose.
Day one ecosystem access · Your brand · Your team · Your mission

Common Questions

Founder FAQs

The questions we hear most often. Answered plainly.

Does Juniper Group acquire minority stakes?
+
No. We acquire majority or full ownership. We're long-term owners, not investors. Minority stakes create misaligned incentives — we prefer to be fully committed to the companies and founders we work with, with clear accountability on both sides.
What size companies does Juniper Group acquire?
+
We look at companies from €1M to €50M+ in annual revenue. Stage and size matter less than strategic fit and team quality. Some of our best acquisitions have been companies that were profitable and focused — not necessarily the biggest in the market.
Do founders stay after acquisition?
+
Absolutely. We love when founders stay to keep building their vision. There's no pressure to exit post-close. Many of our founders have continued to lead and grow their businesses within the Juniper Group ecosystem for years after acquisition. That continuity is one of our greatest strengths.
How long does the acquisition process take?
+
From first call to close, typically 60–120 days. We move at your pace — if you need more time, we respect that. If you're ready to move quickly, we can accelerate. Our process is efficient because we know travel technology deeply and don't spend time learning your industry from scratch.
Is Juniper Group part of a fund?
+
No. We're part of Constellation Software (TSX: CSU) — a publicly traded company on the Toronto Stock Exchange, not a private equity fund with a fixed life. Constellation Software has acquired over 1,100 businesses since 1995 and has never sold one. That track record is what "permanent" actually looks like.
